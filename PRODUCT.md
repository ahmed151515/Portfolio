# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, engineering managers looking to hire a backend engineer, and potential freelance clients looking for API development.

## Product Purpose
To showcase backend engineering skills (ASP.NET Core, clean architecture, scalable APIs) and secure full-time roles or freelance contracts. It should evoke technical expertise, precision, confidence, professionalism, creativity, and delight.

## Brand Personality
Expert, Clean, Modern. It should have the feel of creative portfolios (heavy on animation and visual flair) while maintaining a highly technical and professional tone.

## Anti-references
- Generic Bootstrap/Tailwind templates
- Overly playful or cartoonish designs
- Cluttered layouts with too much text and no visual hierarchy

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence:** The UI should exude the precision and reliability of high-quality backend code.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Use visual flair and smooth animations to demonstrate craft and attention to detail.
- **Clarity Over Clutter:** Maintain strong visual hierarchy, avoiding walls of text to ensure the value proposition is immediately clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility (WCAG AA).
